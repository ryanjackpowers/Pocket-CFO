Pocket Healthcare CFO
Pocket Healthcare CFO is a browser based executive finance intelligence tool designed for healthcare finance leaders.
Purpose
The app helps users:
Research healthcare finance developments
Create executive finance briefings
Evaluate strategic and capital decisions
Analyze financial impact and scenarios
Build decision focused SBARs
Assess margin, cash flow, reimbursement, labor, volume, market share, capital, and liquidity implications
Create custom healthcare finance prompts
